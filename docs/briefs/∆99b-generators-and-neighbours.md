# Generators and neighbours

## Background

Generators and neighbours — a page template whose `for:` key names a field and an `<ambient
value>.<field>` list becomes one page per item at load, its url from its type's default; the
loader fills the `prev`/`next` fields a generated page's type declares, in list order, as
references into the page table (D43). limulus.net's photo and TIL topic pages (`∆ePP`) are
generators
