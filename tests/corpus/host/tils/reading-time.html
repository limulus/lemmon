TIL: schemas

A schema may compute a field from the document it is loaded with: readingTime: duration = body.readingTime() runs once, at load, and every template that shows a chip reads the field rather than counting the words again. The count is of the text between tags, at two hundred words a minute, rounded up — so a note this short is seconds rather than minutes, and a site words that however it likes.
